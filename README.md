**Chief Operating Officer at Geeta Technical Hub (GTH), Geeta University**

I lead technology strategy, operations, and innovation initiatives that connect academic learning with industry-ready skills. With **18+ years** of experience, I focus on building practical AI-driven platforms, scalable digital systems, and high-impact training ecosystems that improve institutional outcomes and student career readiness.

- Industry partnerships, internships, hackathons & placement readiness
- Cloud, Cybersecurity, Data Analytics & operational excellence
